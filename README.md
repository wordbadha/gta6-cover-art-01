Vice Nights Cover Studio — an offline web app that turns any photo and a caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art and exports it as a PNG to share. Pick one of five panel layouts (Vice Grid, Sunset Split, Neon Quartet, Triptych, Poster Solo), fill the panels with your own photos or the built-in samples, crop and position each photo by dragging it on the cover, write the title and subtitle, choose a Vice City colour grade (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Palm Gold or Mono Vice), tune the sun disc, vignette and film grain, and export in the common post sizes: 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080. The lettering is Anton, vendored under the SIL Open Font License. Everything runs from static files under `app/` with no network request, and photos are read on your own machine — nothing is ever uploaded.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, load a photo, and export your cover.
